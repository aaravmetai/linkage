How to play

Find four groups of four MCAT terms that share something. Pick four tiles and hit Submit. You get four mistakes.

  • Every puzzle mixes all sections: Bio/Biochem, Chem/Phys, and Psych/Soc.
  • Watch out. Some tiles look like an easy group but one of them belongs somewhere else.
  • Purple is usually wordplay, like a word that fills in a blank.
  • Each solved group shows a quick explanation so you actually learn something.
  • Sign in with Google to keep your streak on every device. Invite a friend and you both earn bonus tries.
