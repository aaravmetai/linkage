How to play

  • Tap a square, then type. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
  • Every answer has five letters and every clue is MCAT content.
  • Check marks wrong letters in red. Revealing ends the run and does not count toward your streak.
  • A new mini unlocks every midnight. Finish on its own day to keep your streak.

Something is not right

Every square is filled, but at least one letter is wrong. The timer is paused until you hit OK.
